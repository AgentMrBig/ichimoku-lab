Greatest Hits

The five biggest single trades on each pair, ranked by actual dollars banked — sized off the account balance at the moment they fired. Because risk is 1% of the current balance, the fattest tickets are the trades that landed late in the run on an eight-figure account. Every dollar figure is what that one trade added to the stack.

Why these and not the highest-R trades? Dollars ≈ size × R, and size tracks the balance at entry (1% risk, capped at 100 lots per unit). So a plain 2R fade fired on a $27M balance out-earns a 32R monster from early in the run. Almost all of these are pinned at the 100-lot cap — past that point the account is too big for the broker size limit, so the "best" trades cluster near the cap rather than spreading out. R-based backtest (Dukascopy, spread charged); demo model, not realized broker P&L.
