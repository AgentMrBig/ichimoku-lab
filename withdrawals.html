The high‑win‑rate edge (97.6% win, bounded loss) compounding from $2,000, sized at your chosen risk with the ~500‑lot fill ceiling. Then it pays you: once the balance crosses your start line, it withdraws a slice every day — and because the slice is a % of balance, your daily paycheck grows as the machine grows. Drag the sliders. How to drive it. risk/trade sizes each salvo; start paying at is the balance where withdrawals switch on; daily withdrawal is the % of balance you pull every day. A % (not a fixed $) is the trick — it's tiny early so it barely dents growth, then scales into a real paycheck as the account swells. Push the daily % too high and you'll watch the withdrawals outrun the edge and flatten the curve — that's the machine's redline. Research only, M15 model, not the live account.
