How Kaiten uses the clock. The fade hunts the 12:00–15:00 power hours where reversals are cleanest and liquidity is deep. It stands down at the 21:00 rollover (spread guard blocks >3p) and around scheduled data/central-bank windows, because those are the trend/shock days that flip a reversal system upside down. The daily map isn't decoration — it's the hunting schedule. Times are UTC; the 5pm-NY rollover shifts an hour with US daylight saving.
