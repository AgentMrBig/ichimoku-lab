The System

Two independent trading engines running on one always-on server, executing on a single MetaTrader 4 account through a custom Expert Advisor. This is the machine behind the backtest.

Two engines, one account

The edge comes from running two uncorrelated strategies side by side — a fast reversal scalper and a slow trend follower — so their equity curves smooth each other. In backtest the pair was green in 24 of 25 months.

Engine 1 · short-term

Fade 1×9

Catches exhaustion reversals. A signal arms on the M15/M30 structure, then confirms on the 1-minute (9/20 MA convergence → 20-MA break → prior-swing break). Fires 9 units of 1% each at a fixed 2R target.

  • Magic 12345 · 3 pairs
  • Trend-gated: won't fade shorts above the H1 200-MA
  • Backtest edge: 59% win · PF 2.43

Engine 2 · trend

Ichimoku (MTF)

Rides multi-day trends. Daily-bias filter (cloud + Tenkan/Kijun) sets direction; entry on an H4 Tenkan pullback; exit trails the H4 Kijun with a breakeven lock once up +2R.

  • Magic 12347 · gold + EUR + JPY
  • Singles at 1% (the gold "triplet" was tested and dropped)
  • Backtest edge: 39% win · PF 1.34 · the diversifier

A third, dormant component — the NFP grabber (magic 12346) — arms an OCO straddle around high-impact news releases and otherwise sleeps.

How a trade actually happens

The strategy logic lives in Node.js "bridges"; MetaTrader is just the hands that place orders. They talk through files on disk.

MT4 + EA log the ticks. VisionBridgeEA_v11, attached to the 3 charts, writes every tick to ticks_<SYMBOL>.csv in MT4's Files folder.
A bridge reads the ticks and rebuilds the candles it needs (the Ichimoku bridge also splices in backfilled M15 history so it has enough bars from the first second).
The bridge computes a signal — reversal confluence for the fade, daily-bias + pullback for Ichimoku — and applies its gates (trend filter, per-pair cooldown, one-position rule).
It writes an order file (orders_<SYMBOL>.json) with side, stop, risk %, and units — and pings Discord.
The EA executes it next tick: sizes the lots off the live balance, opens the position(s) with the right magic number.
The EA manages the position — trails the Kijun, locks breakeven at +2R, banks/runs — all keyed by magic number, independent of the bridges.

Because risk is a percent of the current balance, wins compound — which is where the backtest's steep curve comes from.

The stack

Everything lives on one small always-on Linux VPS so trades never miss when the home PC is off. MetaTrader (a Windows app) runs under Wine; the bridges run natively.

Hostinger KVM · Ubuntu 24.04 Wine 9 · MT4 portable VisionBridgeEA_v11 · the EA Node.js · the two bridges pm2 · process manager Xvfb + noVNC · headless display

Every piece is a boot-persistent service — a reboot brings the whole stack back automatically, MT4 auto-logs-in, and the engines resume. Remote control is command-line over SSH; the MT4 desktop is reachable in a browser through a tunneled noVNC session.
